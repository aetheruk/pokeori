# Trainer Battle Win-Line Worksheet

This worksheet contains **261 authored PvE trainer battles**. Wild battles, PvP formats, and the explicitly marked Test battle are excluded.

Areas follow a rough Kanto play order. Within each area, entries retain their authored order; use the context to place side-story, route, Gym, and expedition rematches. Optional summons and Gym Leader Chronicles are grouped at the end. Expedition variants and other distinct battle IDs are listed separately, even when they reuse a trainer or team.

Each **Battle ID** maps to one authored battle config. **Context** comes from its description and area. Existing custom lines are copied exactly from `winMessage`; “None authored” means the battle currently uses shared result dialogue. Fill in **New win line** for each battle. I’ll use the completed fields to update matching battle configs; an unchanged `_Write here._` placeholder means no change.
