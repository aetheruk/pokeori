# Kanto Gym Leader Chronicles

The canonical narrative treatments, voice rules, exact beat orders, and continuity guardrails for the eight Badge Chronicles live in [`docs/game-data/gym-leader-chronicles-story-bible.md`](../game-data/gym-leader-chronicles-story-bible.md).

This design deliberately keeps narrative planning separate from expedition mechanics: each complete treatment is approved first, then encoded as one ordered sequence of scene, game, and battle beats. The runtime derives task entries, activity pools, and expedition paths from that sequence so authored dialogue cannot drift away from gameplay order.

All Badge Chronicles remain fixed, secret, one-time memories unlocked independently through Spirit Channeling in Pokémon Tower. They preserve their Badge rituals, expedition IDs, Leader parties, reward values, and Gym Heroes daily effects.
