# Gen I Trainer Walker Sheets

`trainer_GB_RED.png` and `trainer_GB_BLUE.png` are 4×4 directional walker sheets adapted from the six 16×16 poses in the Pokémon Red/Blue disassembly:

- Red: https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/master/gfx/sprites/red.png
- Blue: https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/master/gfx/sprites/blue.png

The original down, up, and left poses are paired with their walking poses; the right-facing row mirrors the left-facing frames. The transparent background and each trainer's Red or Blue palette are retained in the app's sheet format.
