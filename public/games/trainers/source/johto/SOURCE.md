# Johto trainer overworld sprites

Professor Elm's HGSS directional walking sheet comes from PurpleZaffre's ULTIMATE Gen 4 Overworlds Pack (Eevee Expo resource 609). The pack includes all official DPPt and HGSS human overworld sprites, aligned for RPG Maker XP; credit requested to PurpleZaffre.

Source: https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/609/

Elm's four-direction sheet is 256×256 px (four columns × four rows, 64×64 px per frame) in down/left/right/up row order. The static trainer portrait uses the first down-facing frame from this sheet. Bulbapedia identifies the corresponding Generation IV overworld sprite as Professor Elm: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/File:Elm_OD.png
