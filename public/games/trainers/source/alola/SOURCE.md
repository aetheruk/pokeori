# Alola leader overworld sprites

Sheets from Droid779's **Pokémon SM/USUM Characters and Trainers OW Sprites in Gen III Style** resource (Eevee Expo resource 284). The author requests credit to Droid779.

Source: https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/284/

The source sheets are 128×192 px (four columns × four rows, 32×48 px per frame) and use the app's down/left/right/up row order.
