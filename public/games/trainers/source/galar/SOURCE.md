# Galar Gym Leader overworld sprites

Directional walking sheets from the Pokéngine trainer profiles in its Galar Collection. The profiles identify DiegoWT as the overworld sprite artist and mark the trainer sprites free to use with credit. Milo and Nessa are listed as commissions by LoganPaul0.

Collection: https://pokengine.org/collections/10v7cb8p/Galar?tab=trainers

Mapped profile IDs:

- Allister `0115fddl`
- Bea `01a5bwe7`
- Gordie `01zuecx4`
- Kabu `01w7a5vi`
- Melony `01klfd6q`
- Milo `01o9if40`
- Nessa `01ipzmzp`
- Opal `01csl0fi`
- Piers `01pgheek`
- Raihan `01km6ox3`

Each sheet is 96×128 px: three 32×32 frames across and four direction rows.
